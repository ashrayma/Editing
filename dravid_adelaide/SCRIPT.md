# Adelaide 2003: 556 and Still Lost
### YouTube Short / Instagram Reel · 9:16 · ~80 s · English voiceover, English on-screen text

Read one paragraph at a time with a short pause between them.

---

Australia made 556... and still lost.

Ponting's 242 meant nothing. Because standing in the way was Rahul Dravid. 305 runs. Out only once.

And before this Test? Eight innings in Australia. Just 137 runs.

December 2003. Steve Waugh's final series. India hadn't won a Test in Australia for 22 years.

Day one: Australia 400 for 5. They finished on 556.

India's reply? 85 for 4. And the captain, Ganguly, run out... in a mix-up with Dravid. Dravid later said that guilt was burning inside him.

Then came Laxman. The same pair that stopped Australia at Kolkata in 2001. A 303-run partnership. Dravid batted for almost ten hours.

Ponting 242. Dravid 233. India just 33 behind.

Then Ajit Agarkar. 6 for 41. The team that made 556... bowled out for 196.

Target: 230. Final day, one after another: Sehwag, Sachin, Ganguly, Laxman. At the other end, Dravid didn't budge. 72 not out.

And the winning shot? A square cut off MacGill. Four.

After the match, Steve Waugh walked over... and handed Dravid that ball.

305 runs. Nearly 14 hours at the crease.

Dravid or Ponting? Next: Kolkata 2001.
