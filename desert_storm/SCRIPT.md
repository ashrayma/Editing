# Voiceover script (Hinglish, conversational)

Tell it like you're telling a friend a story. The hook clip plays first (the host's question and Virat's "Sharjah 98, Desert Storm"), and you start right after it.

**How to record**
- One take, phone voice memo is fine. Quiet room, phone 15–20 cm from your mouth.
- Read the lines **in order**, with a short breath (about half a second) between lines. That pause is how the edit finds each line, so don't run two lines together.
- Talk, don't read: natural ups and downs, a smile on the questions ("Ab aap bologe…", "Birthday gift?"), a slight pause before **asli toofan**.
- Lean on the **bold** words; that's where the cards and highlights land.
- Changing a word here and there is fine; don't skip or reorder lines.
- Send the file as it is (m4a / mp3 / wav). No need to trim or clean it.

## The story in one read

Haan, Virat Kohli bhi chahte hain ki kaash ye innings unhone kheli hoti. Toh chalo, aapko le chalta hoon Sharjah, 1998 mein. Tareekh thi 22 April. Coca-Cola Cup chal raha tha, aur India ko kaise bhi final mein pahunchna tha. Pehle batting ki Australia ne, aur bana diye 284 runs. Ab aap bologe, 284 mein kya bada hai? Par yaar, us zamaane mein 230 bhi bada score maana jaata tha. Aur saamne thi late 90s ki Australia. Woh team jo kisi ko bhi, kahin bhi hara deti thi. India chase kar raha tha, ki tabhi kuch aisa hua jo kisi ne socha bhi nahi tha. Ek zabardast sandstorm aaya, aur poora stadium dhool mein chhup gaya. Pachchees minute khel ruka raha. Jab dhool baithi, toh target ho gaya 46 overs mein 276. Kaam aur mushkil ho gaya. Aur yahin se shuru hua asli toofan. Sachin Tendulkar ne 131 balls pe 143 runs thok diye. Nau chauke, paanch chhakke. Bowler koi bhi ho, sabki dhulaai hui. Match toh India haar gaya, par Sachin ne akele dum pe India ko final mein pahuncha diya. Aur kahani yahin khatam nahi hoti. Do din baad final tha, aur us din Sachin ka 25th birthday tha. Birthday gift? Ek aur century. 134 runs, aur India ne cup jeet liya. Shane Warne ne baad mein mazaak mein kaha, ki ab toh sapne mein bhi Sachin unhe chhakke maarte dikhenge. Toh ab samajh aaya na, Virat ko yahi innings kyun chahiye thi? Aur aap? Agar ek innings choose karni ho, toh kaunsi? Comment mein batao.

## Line by line

| 2 | Tareekh thi 22 April. Coca-Cola Cup chal raha tha, aur India ko kaise bhi **final** mein pahunchna tha. | 22 April. The Coca-Cola Cup. And India needed a place in the final. |

| 5 | Aur saamne thi late 90s ki Australia. Woh team jo **kisi ko bhi, kahin bhi** hara deti thi. | And this was late-90s Australia. A team that beat anyone, anywhere. |
| 6 | India chase kar raha tha, ki tabhi kuch aisa hua jo **kisi ne socha bhi nahi tha**. | India were chasing, when something nobody expected happened. |
| 7 | Ek zabardast **sandstorm** aaya, aur poora stadium dhool mein chhup gaya. Pachchees minute khel ruka raha. | A huge sandstorm hit. The stadium vanished in dust. Play stopped for 25 minutes. |

| 9 | Aur yahin se shuru hua **asli toofan**. | And that's where the real storm began. |
| 10 | Sachin Tendulkar ne 131 balls pe **143 runs** thok diye. | Sachin Tendulkar smashed 143 off 131 balls. |
| 11 | Nau chauke, **paanch chhakke**. Bowler koi bhi ho, sabki dhulaai hui. | Nine fours, five sixes. No bowler was spared. |
| 12 | Match toh India haar gaya, par Sachin ne **akele dum pe** India ko final mein pahuncha diya. | India lost the match, but Sachin single-handedly took them to the final. |
| 13 | Aur kahani yahin khatam nahi hoti. Do din baad final tha, aur us din Sachin ka **25th birthday** tha. | And it doesn't end there. Two days later came the final, on Sachin's 25th birthday. |
| 14 | Birthday gift? **Ek aur century**. 134 runs, aur India ne **cup jeet liya**. | His birthday gift? Another century. 134, and India won the cup. |
| 15 | Shane Warne ne baad mein mazaak mein kaha, ki ab toh **sapne mein bhi** Sachin unhe chhakke maarte dikhenge. | Shane Warne later joked he'd have nightmares of Sachin hitting him for six. |
| 16 | Toh ab samajh aaya na, Virat ko **yahi innings** kyun chahiye thi? | Now you get why Virat wishes this one was his. |
| 17 | Aur aap? Agar ek innings choose karni ho, toh **kaunsi**? Comment mein batao. | And you? If you could pick one innings, which one? Tell me in the comments. |

About 80–90 seconds at a relaxed, story-telling pace.
