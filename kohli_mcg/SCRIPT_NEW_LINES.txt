Us raat Mohali mein India ko chahiye the 161. Aur score tha 49 for 3. Saamne Australia ka poora pace attack.

Phir Kohli ne target ko tukdon mein tod diya. Har single ko double banaya. Dhoni ke saath wicket ke beech aise daude, jaise har run aakhri ho.

18 gendon mein 39 chahiye the. Aur Kohli ne Faulkner ko chauke pe chauka maara.

Dhoni ne chauke se match khatam kiya. Aur Kohli wahin pitch pe ghutno ke bal baith gaye.

Wahi Kohli. Wahi ghutne. Wahi zameen. Chhe saal baad, MCG pe, yahi scene phir se hone wala tha.

Haar ke baad social media pe Mohammed Shami ko unke dharm ki wajah se troll kiya gaya.

Kohli, jo khud captaincy aur form dono se lad rahe the, khul ke Shami ke saath khade hue.

Unhone kaha, kisi ko uske dharm ki wajah se nishana banana, sabse ghatiya cheez hai jo ek insaan kar sakta hai.

Stadium ka ek hissa neela tha, ek hissa hara. Dono taraf se naare. Aisa mahaul, jaise yeh World Cup ka final ho.

Australia ki tez pitch, Pakistan ka pace, aur 90 hazaar logon ka shor. India ke top order ke paas koi jawab nahi tha.

Kohli aur Hardik ne 113 runs ki partnership ki. Ek taraf Hardik bade shot dhoondh rahe the, doosri taraf Kohli ek ek run ke liye daud rahe the.

Aur is shot ke liye sirf technique nahi, himmat chahiye thi. Gend thodi bhi idhar udhar hoti, toh catch tha. Aur match khatam.

Lekin us ek pal mein, Kohli ne teen saal ka saara gussa, saara dard, us ek shot mein daal diya.

Rule yeh hai ki free hit pe batsman sirf run out ho sakta hai. Stumps se lagne ke baad bhi gend live rehti hai. Isliye woh teen runs count hue.

Hardik Pandya ki aankhon mein bhi aansu the. Unhone yeh jeet apne pita ko dedicate ki, jo ab is duniya mein nahi the.
