# 31 for 4: The Night Kohli Came Back
### Recording script · YouTube long-form · ~15 min

**How to record:** read each numbered line, then pause for a second before the next. If you fumble, just say the line again; I'll cut the bad take. One long recording or one file per chapter both work. "(...)" means a short dramatic pause inside the line.

## COLD OPEN (0:00 – 0:50)

1. 23 October 2022. Melbourne. 90 hazaar log stadium mein. Aur India ko chahiye... 8 gendon mein 28 runs.
2. Saamne Haris Rauf. Us din Pakistan ka sabse khatarnaak bowler. 3 overs, sirf 21 runs, 2 wickets.
3. Crease pe ek aisa aadmi, jiske baare mein teen saal se duniya keh rahi thi ki... uska time khatam ho gaya.
4. Aur phir agli do gendon mein jo hua, use aaj bhi log "shot of the century" kehte hain.
5. Lekin un do gendon ki kahani Melbourne mein shuru nahi hoti. Woh shuru hoti hai teen saal pehle... jab Virat Kohli ke bat se runs aana band ho gaye.
6. Yeh kahani hai ek hazaar bees din ki khamoshi ki. Aur ek raat ki, jisne sab kuch badal diya.

## CHAPTER 1 · THE KING (0:50 – 2:20)

7. Is khamoshi ko samajhne ke liye, pehle yeh samajhna zaroori hai ki Kohli kitni oonchai se gire the.
8. 2014. England. Das innings, sirf 134 runs. Log keh rahe the, swing ke saamne Kohli ki technique nahi chalti.
9. Kohli ne jawab mein apni body badli, apni diet badli, apna game badla.
10. 2016 IPL mein, ek season mein 973 runs. Chaar centuries. Aaj tak koi us record ke paas nahi pahuncha.
11. Usi saal Mohali. T20 World Cup, Australia ke khilaaf, jeetna zaroori. Kohli ke 51 gendon mein 82 not out. Kohli ne khud kaha tha, yeh meri best innings hai. Yeh line yaad rakhiyega. Kahani ke end mein wapas aayegi.
12. 2018. Wapas England. Wahi swing, wahi Anderson. Is baar 593 runs.
13. Aur November 2019 tak, Virat Kohli ke naam the 70 international centuries. Sirf Sachin aur Ricky Ponting unse aage the. Aur Kohli sirf 31 saal ke the.
14. Duniya maan chuki thi, Sachin ka 100 centuries ka record agar koi todega, toh yahi aadmi todega.
15. Lekin 70th century ke baad... kuch aisa hua, jiska kisi ko andaaza nahi tha.

## CHAPTER 2 · THE SILENCE (2:20 – 5:00)

17. Kisi ne nahi socha tha ki agli century ke liye teen saal lagenge.

19. Aur jab cricket wapas aaya, Kohli ka bat chal raha tha, par century nahi aa rahi thi. 50 aate, 60 aate, 70 aate. Aur phir... out.
20. December 2020. Adelaide. Kohli ne 74 banaye, run out hue. Aur doosri innings mein India 36 pe all out. Test history ka India ka sabse kam score.
21. 2021 aaya. Century phir bhi nahi aayi.
22. September 2021. Kohli ne announce kiya, World Cup ke baad woh T20 captaincy chhod denge.
23. Aur phir aaya 24 October 2021. Dubai. T20 World Cup. India versus Pakistan.
24. Shaheen Afridi ne Rohit aur Rahul ko shuru mein hi bhej diya. Kohli ne akele 57 banaye. Lekin Babar aur Rizwan ne ek bhi wicket nahi gira, aur 10 wickets se match jeet liya.
25. World Cup history mein pehli baar, Pakistan ne India ko haraya. Aur captain the, Virat Kohli.
26. December 2021. ODI captaincy bhi chali gayi. January 2022. South Africa mein series haarne ke baad, Kohli ne Test captaincy bhi chhod di.
27. Saat saal tak jo aadmi India ka chehra tha, ab uske paas na captaincy thi, na century.
28. IPL 2022 mein teen baar golden duck. Pehli hi gend pe out. Teen baar.
29. Headlines badal gayi. "Rest karo." "Team se nikalo." "Kohli khatam ho gaya."
30. Aur andar kya chal raha tha, yeh Kohli ne baad mein khud bataya. Unhone kaha, main apni intensity fake kar raha tha. Andar se main thak chuka tha.
31. Unhone yeh bhi bataya ki jab unhone Test captaincy chhodi, toh unke purane teammates mein se sirf ek insaan ka message aaya. MS Dhoni.
32. (pause) Aur phir Kohli ne woh kiya, jo unhone pichle das saal mein kabhi nahi kiya tha.

## CHAPTER 3 · THE RETURN (5:00 – 6:40)

37. Kohli muskura rahe the. Bahut time baad, woh cricket enjoy karte dikh rahe the.
38. 8 September 2022. Afghanistan ke khilaaf. Kohli opening karne aaye.
39. 61 gendon mein 122 not out. Barah chauke, chhe chhakke. Unke career ki pehli T20 international century.
40. Aur century number 71. Ek hazaar bees din baad.

## CHAPTER 4 · THE STAGE (6:40 – 8:20)

44. T20 World Cup 2022. Australia. India ka pehla match. Aur saamne Pakistan.

46. 23 October. Diwali se ek din pehle. Melbourne mein poore hafte baarish ka forecast tha. Lekin us shaam aasmaan saaf ho gaya.
47. Melbourne Cricket Ground. Stadium mein 90,293 log. Aur TV pe crore log.
48. Rohit Sharma ne toss jeeta, aur pehle bowling chuni. National anthem ke time Rohit ki aankhein bhar aayi thi.
49. Aur pehle hi over mein Arshdeep Singh. Babar Azam, pehli gend. LBW. Zero.
50. Phir Rizwan. Arshdeep ki short ball, aur Bhuvi ka catch. 15 for 2.
51. Lekin Pakistan ne wapsi ki. Iftikhar Ahmed ne 34 gendon mein 51 thoke. Shan Masood aakhri tak tike rahe, 52 not out.
52. Hardik Pandya ke 3 wickets, Arshdeep ke 3. Pakistan ruka 159 pe.
53. 160 chahiye. MCG ki badi boundaries. Aur saamne duniya ka sabse tez pace attack. Shaheen, Naseem, Rauf.

## CHAPTER 7 · 28 OFF 8 (11:00 – 12:40)

70. 19th over. Haris Rauf. Pehli chaar gendon mein sirf 3 runs.
71. 8 gendein. 28 runs.
72. Gharon mein log TV band kar rahe the. Kuch ne bola, "Ho gaya, khatam." Kuch so gaye.
73. (pause) Paanchvi gend. Rauf ki back of a length delivery, 145 ke paas ki speed.
74. Kohli back foot pe gaye. Na koi slog, na koi swing. Bas bat ka seedha face. Aur gend... seedha bowler ke sir ke upar se. Sightscreen ke paas. Six.

76. Baad mein khud Haris Rauf ne kaha, agar yeh shot Dinesh Karthik ya Hardik maarte, toh unhe bura lagta. Lekin yeh Kohli tha. Aur Kohli aise shot maar sakta hai.

## CHAPTER 8 · THE LAST OVER (12:40 – 14:20)

80. Gend Mohammad Nawaz ke haath mein. Wahi spinner, jiska over bachaya gaya tha.

92. Aur India jeet gaya!
