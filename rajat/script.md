## Recording tips
- Phone close to your mouth (one hand away), quiet room with soft furnishings, no fan or AC.
- Read each numbered line as its own sentence with a 1-second pause between lines.
- If you fumble, pause, then repeat the whole line. I cut the mistakes out.
- Energy: a letter to a friend. Calm, a little disappointed in line 10, punchy on "Selectors don't wait."
