# Dear Rajat (Reel / Short, 9:16, ~55 s, English VO, spoken directly to Rajat)

Facts checked 11 Nov 2026: BCCI announcement 8 Nov (withdrawn from India A, Rising Stars Asia Cup;
asked not to be considered for 50-over cricket while managing injuries; Rinku Singh vice-captain,
Ayush Badoni replacement). MP named Patidar SMAT captain 7 Nov; SMAT starts 14 Nov. Born 1 Jun 1993 (33).

Dear Rajat, can I ask you something honestly? Do you know what you just did? You said no to India.
You told the BCCI not to pick you for fifty-over cricket, because of your injuries. Fair enough. Your body,
your call. But Rajat... six days later, you're leading Madhya Pradesh in a T20 tournament. So tell me, is it
really the injury? Or have you already picked your format? I get it, I really do. You're thirty-three. You
lifted the IPL trophy for RCB, something nobody before you could do. Maybe T20 is where you feel at home now.
But please remember, selectors don't wait for anyone. Rinku is already vice-captain. Badoni already has your
spot. You fought for years for that India cap, Rajat. Don't let it slip away quietly. I really hope this is
a plan, and not a goodbye. Because at thirty-three, my friend, time isn't on your side.

Smart move, or big mistake? Tell him in one word in the comments, and follow for the next letter.

~165 words = about 58-60 s.

## Recording tips
- Talk to him, don't read to us: imagine Rajat is sitting across from you.
- Pause before "But Rajat...", "So tell me" and "Don't let it slip away quietly."
- Softer and warmer on "I really hope this is a plan"; firm on "selectors don't wait for anyone."
- Fumble? Pause, then repeat the whole sentence. I cut the mistakes out.
