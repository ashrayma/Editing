# PRITHVI SHAW — INDIA'S NEXT SACHIN… WHAT HAPPENED TO HIM?
The Cricket Lounge | Untold Stories #6 | ~8–10 min. Script as supplied by the creator (narration lines only).

## OPENING
14 saal ki age mein 546 runs.
18 saal ki age mein India ke liye Test debut… aur pehli hi innings mein century.
Us waqt duniya ko laga tha — India ko apna next superstar mil gaya hai.
Sachin Tendulkar se comparison hone lagi. Future captain tak ki baatein hone lagi.
Lekin kuch hi saalon mein… Wahi Prithvi Shaw international cricket se door ho gaya.
Na India ki jersey. Na IPL auction mein buyers. Aur na hi woh superstar status, jiska kabhi poora desh intezaar kar raha tha.
Aakhir aisa kya hua Prithvi Shaw ke saath?

## CH1 — THE PRODIGY
November 2013. Mumbai ka Harris Shield tournament. Ek 14 saal ka ladka batting karne aata hai.
Aur phir jo hota hai, woh school cricket ki history mein yaad rakha jaata hai.
Prithvi Shaw scores 546 runs. Ek innings mein 546 runs!
Uski batting mein ek alag hi confidence tha. Front foot par aakar shots khelna, bowlers par attack karna… aur itni chhoti age mein game ko samajhne ka tareeka.
Logon ko lagne laga tha ki Indian cricket ko ek extraordinary talent mil chuka hai.
2018 mein Shaw ne India Under-19 team ko World Cup jitaya. Aur us team mein ek aur young batter tha — Shubman Gill.
Dono young stars the. Dono se bahut expectations thi. Lekin Prithvi ka international debut Gill se pehle aa gaya.
October 4, 2018. Rajkot. India vs West Indies. Prithvi Shaw makes his Test debut. Aur apni pehli hi innings mein century! 134 runs.
Woh Test debut par century banane wale youngest Indian batters mein se ek ban gaya.
Uski batting dekhkar laga ki international cricket mein bhi woh school aur domestic cricket ki tarah hi dominate karega.
Media mein comparison shuru ho gayi. India's next Sachin.
Lekin ek young cricketer ke liye sabse bada challenge debut par century banana nahi hota. The real challenge is staying there.
Aur Prithvi ke career mein pehla bada setback aane wala tha.

## CH2 — THE INJURY
November 2018. India Australia tour par thi. Prithvi Shaw ko apni pehli overseas Test series khelne ka mauka milne wala tha.
Lekin practice match ke warm-up ke dauraan uske ankle mein injury ho gayi. Injury itni serious thi ki woh poori Test series se bahar ho gaya.
Ek taraf India Australia mein history banane ki koshish kar rahi thi. Aur doosri taraf, Prithvi apne comeback ki preparation kar raha tha.
Injuries kisi bhi cricketer ke career ka hissa hoti hain. Lekin ek young player ke liye timing bahut important hoti hai.
International cricket mein experience gain karne ka mauka milta hai, confidence build hota hai, aur team mein apni jagah banane ka time milta hai.
Prithvi ka momentum yahin interrupt ho gaya. Aur agla setback sirf injury nahi tha.

## CH3 — THE SUSPENSION
July 2019. BCCI ne announce kiya ki Prithvi Shaw ka dope test positive aaya hai. Uske sample mein terbutaline naam ka banned substance detect hua.
Shaw ne explain kiya ki usne cough medicine li thi aur banned substance accidentally uske body mein chala gaya.
BCCI ne inadvertent ingestion ko accept kiya aur us par eight-month backdated suspension lagaya.
Lekin is suspension ka impact sirf kuch matches miss karne tak limited nahi tha.
Prithvi ek aise stage par tha jahan usse regular cricket khelkar apni position establish karni thi. Lekin ab woh cricket se door tha.
Aur isi beech uske saath Under-19 World Cup jeetne wale players aage badh rahe the. Shubman Gill ko dekhiye.
Dono ne 2018 mein saath World Cup jeeta tha. Lekin Gill dheere-dheere Indian team mein apni jagah establish kar raha tha.
Same generation. Same starting point. But very different journeys.
Aur phir aaya December 2020. Woh match, jisne Prithvi Shaw ke Test career ki direction badal di.

## CH4 — ADELAIDE
December 2020. India Australia mein thi. Adelaide mein pink-ball Test. Prithvi Shaw ko playing eleven mein jagah mili.
Lekin uski batting mein woh confidence nazar nahi aaya, jisne use international cricket tak pahunchaya tha.
First innings — 0. Second innings — 4. Dono innings mein woh bowled hua.
Aur phir aaya woh moment jo Indian cricket history mein hamesha yaad rakha jayega. India all out for 36. Test cricket mein India ka lowest-ever total.
Is defeat ke baad team management ne changes kiye. Prithvi Shaw dropped ho gaya. Aur uske baad… He never played another Test for India.
Socho. Jis player ne Test debut par 134 banaye the, uska Test career sirf kuch matches ke baad ruk gaya.
Lekin kya yeh kahani yahin khatam ho gayi thi? Bilkul nahi. Prithvi ke paas abhi bhi talent tha. Aur usne domestic cricket mein iska proof bhi diya.

## CH5 — THE COMEBACKS
2020–21 Vijay Hazare Trophy. Prithvi Shaw ne Mumbai ke liye 827 runs score kiye. Ek tournament mein 827 runs!
Ismein ek unforgettable 227 not out ki innings bhi thi. Usne Mumbai ko title jitane mein important role play kiya.
Yeh ek statement tha. Prithvi abhi khatam nahi hua tha.
Phir 2023 mein England ke Northamptonshire ke liye usne 244 runs ki explosive innings kheli. 153 balls. 244 runs.
Ek baar phir duniya ko uska talent yaad aa gaya. Whenever Shaw got going, he looked unstoppable.
Lekin yahan ek problem thi. Uske career mein flashes of brilliance toh bahut the… Lekin woh un performances ko consistent international success mein convert nahi kar paa raha tha.
Aur isi beech uske career ke around controversies bhi badhne lagi.

## CH6 — THE CONTROVERSY (2023)
February 2023. Mumbai. Prithvi Shaw apne friends ke saath ek hotel mein dinner kar raha tha.
Wahan kuch log uske saath selfies aur videos lena chahte the.
Shaw ke friend ki complaint ke mutabik, Prithvi ne initially selfies di, lekin repeated requests ke baad mana kar diya.
Uske baad argument hua. Hotel management ne un logon ko bahar jaane ke liye kaha. Lekin controversy yahin khatam nahi hui.
Police complaint ke mutabik, hotel ke bahar Shaw ki car par baseball bat se attack hua. Baad mein uske friend ki car ka windshield bhi damage hua.
Complaint mein extortion aur threats ke allegations bhi lagaye gaye. Sapna Gill aur kuch doosre log arrest hue.
Lekin doosri taraf Gill ne bhi Shaw aur uske friends par assault ke counter-allegations lagaye.
June 2023 mein police ne court ko bataya ki investigation mein Gill ki molestation allegations false paayi gayi thi. Police ne CCTV footage aur witness statements ka hawala diya.
Yahan ek baat clear honi chahiye. Prithvi ka selfies dene se mana karna koi galti nahi thi. He had every right to say no.
Lekin is incident ne uske career ke around ek aur controversy create kar di.
Aur jab aap already apni cricketing position ko lekar struggle kar rahe hote hain, toh aisi distractions aapke liye aur difficult ho sakti hain.
Lekin Prithvi ke career ka sabse bada sawaal abhi bhi uski cricketing performance aur professionalism ko lekar tha.

## CH7 — THE FALL
October 2024. Mumbai ne Prithvi Shaw ko Ranji Trophy squad se drop kar diya. Officially, use break diya gaya tha.
Lekin reports mein fitness aur discipline se related concerns saamne aaye.
November 2024. IPL mega auction. Ek waqt tha jab franchises uske liye bidding kar rahi thi. Lekin is baar… Prithvi Shaw unsold reh gaya. No franchise picked him.
Aur December 2024 mein Mumbai Cricket Association ke ek official ne PTI ko bataya ki Shaw ke fitness aur fielding standards par concerns the.
Unke statement ka ek hissa tha: "10 fielders ke saath cricket nahi khela ja sakta."
Yeh ek harsh statement tha. Lekin isne ek important question raise kiya. Kya sirf batting talent enough hai?
Modern cricket mein aapko runs ke saath fitness, fielding, discipline aur consistency bhi dikhani padti hai.
Shaw ne is criticism ka jawab social media par diya: "Full opinions, with half facts."
Lekin cricket mein jawab sirf social media posts se nahi diya jaata. Aakhir mein jawab performance se hi dena padta hai.
Aur Prithvi ke saamne ab sabse bada challenge yahi tha. Toh kya ab uska career khatam ho chuka tha?

## CH8 — THE ROAD BACK
2025 mein Prithvi Shaw ne Mumbai chhodkar Maharashtra ke liye khelna shuru kiya.
Yeh ek fresh start tha. Ek nayi team. Naya environment. Aur apne career ko dobara build karne ka ek aur chance.
Lekin India mein comeback karna itna simple nahi hai. Competition bahut zyada hai. Domestic cricket mein har season naye players perform kar rahe hain.
Aur national team mein jagah banane ke liye sirf ek achhi innings kaafi nahi hoti.
Prithvi ko ab regular runs banane honge. Apni fitness prove karni hogi.
Aur sabse important — selectors ko yeh dikhana hoga ki woh long-term commitment ke saath apna career rebuild kar raha hai.
Uska India ke liye dobara khelne ka dream abhi bhi zinda hai. Lekin dream aur comeback ke beech ka distance performances se hi cover hoga.

## FINAL CHAPTER
Toh aakhir Prithvi Shaw ke saath hua kya?
Kya woh injuries ki wajah se peeche reh gaya? Kya suspension ne uska momentum tod diya? Kya Adelaide ka failure uske confidence par bhaari pada?
Ya phir fitness, discipline aur consistency ke questions ne uske career ko aur difficult bana diya?
Shayad iska jawab in sab cheezon mein chhupa hai. Lekin ek baat zaroor hai.
Prithvi Shaw ki story ko sirf failure ki story kehna galat hoga.
Kuch setbacks uske control mein nahi the. Kuch moments mein usne khud bhi opportunities miss ki. Aur kuch expectations shayad us par bahut jaldi daal di gayi thi.
At 18, he was called the next Sachin.
Lekin shayad kisi ne yeh nahi socha ki 18 saal ke ek ladke ke liye itni expectations ko carry karna kitna difficult ho sakta hai.
Cricket ne usse talent diya. Lekin ab usse apne career ko dobara banane ke liye patience, discipline aur consistency dikhani hogi.
Because talent can open the door. But consistency keeps you inside.
Prithvi Shaw is still young enough to make a comeback.
Sawaal bas itna hai… Kya woh apne talent ko ek baar phir Indian jersey tak le jaa payega?
Ya phir cricket history mein uska naam ek aise player ke roop mein reh jayega… Jiske paas superstar banne ka talent toh tha… Lekin woh superstar ban nahi paaya?
