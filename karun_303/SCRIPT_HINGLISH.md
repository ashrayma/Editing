Test cricket mein teen sau run — India ke liye sirf do aadmiyon ne banaye hain. Ek, Virender Sehwag. Aur doosra… ek aisa naam, jo shayad aapko yaad bhi nahi.
Us doosre aadmi ne uske baad teen Test khele. Bas teen.
Jisne 381 gendein kheli… jise out karne ke teen mauke England ne gawaye… aur har galti ki keemat unhe chukaani padi… jiske liye us raat har channel par ek hi baat thi — "ye agle das saal Team India ka number paanch hai"…
Wo aadmi teen mahine baad apna aakhri Test khel raha tha — agle aath saal ke liye.
Aur sabse buri baat — use khud pata nahi tha.

Ye number aapne saikdon baar suna hai — 303.
Aur har baar ek hi kahani sunayi gayi hai —
"Karun ko drop kar diya gaya. Bada anyaay hua."
Nahi. Wo poori kahani nahi hai. Drop hona toh sirf pehla kadam tha.
Wo 303 unka bachaav nahi tha. Wo unki saza thi.
Aur aath saal baad, jab wo wapas aaye — toh 303 bhi unke saath wapas aaya.

## [2:00] CHENNAI, 303*
ON SCREEN: "TEST DEBUT · MOHALI · NOV 2016" → scores 4 · 13 → "CHENNAI · DEC 2016 · 5TH TEST vs ENGLAND" → "DROPPED ON 34" → "100 OFF 185 BALLS" → "KL RAHUL 199" → **303\*** "381 BALLS · 32 FOURS · 4 SIXES" → "INDIA 759/7 DEC" → "WON BY AN INNINGS & 75 · SERIES 4-0" → Sehwag quote card → **TEST AVERAGE: 160**

November 2016. Karun ka Test debut hua. Mohali mein 4. Mumbai mein 13.
Shuruaat dheemi thi. Kisi ne khaas dhyaan nahi diya.
Phir aaya December. Chennai. England ke khilaaf paanchva Test. Rahane chotil the — toh Karun phir khele.
34 ke score par Alastair Cook ne slip mein unka catch chhoda.
Aur wahin se sab badal gaya.
185 gendon mein pehla Test shatak.
Doosre chhor par KL Rahul — 199 par out hue. Ek run se double century chooki.
Lekin Karun nahi ruke.
Tea ke baad double century. Phir Joe Root ne 217 par ek aur catch chhoda.
Aur phir Adil Rashid ki gend par chauka — aur score tha teen sau.
381 gendein. 303 run. Not out. 32 chauke, 4 chhakke.
India ne 759 par pari declare ki — us waqt India ka sabse bada Test score.
India ne match pari aur 75 run se jeeta. Series 4–0.
Virender Sehwag ne likha — "Welcome to the 300 club. Baarah saal aath mahine se yahan bahut akela tha."
Sehwag ke baad triple century banane wale — sirf doosre Indian.
Aur ye unka pehla hi shatak tha. Pehle shatak ko teen sau mein badalne wale — duniya mein sirf teesre batsman. Garry Sobers aur Bob Simpson ke baad.
Teen Test ke baad unka Test average tha — 160.

Chennai se pehle wo ek yuva batsman the. Chennai ke baad wo "wo ladka jisne 303 banaye" the.
Farq samjhiye.
Ek yuva batsman se ummeed hoti hai — run banao.
Ek 303 wale batsman se ummeed hoti hai — saabit karo ki wo ek din nahi tha.
Har pari mein do sawaal jud gaye.
Pehla — run kyun nahi bane.
Doosra — teen sau kyun nahi bane.
303 unka taaj nahi tha. 303 unka benchmark tha.
Aur benchmark wo hota hai, jise har baar chhoona padta hai.

## [5:15] THE VERY NEXT TEST
ON SCREEN: "HYDERABAD · 9 FEB 2017 · vs BANGLADESH" → **BENCHED** stamp → "HUTTON · INZAMAM: INJURED" / "KARUN: FIT" → quote cards: FATHER: "FAIR TO PICK RAHANE." · KARUN: "ONE GAME DOESN'T OVERSHADOW TWO YEARS OF HARD WORK."

Aur phir aaya agla Test. Bangladesh. Hyderabad. February 2017.
303 ke theek baad wala Test.
Aur Karun Nair — playing eleven mein nahi the.

Captain Virat Kohli ne match se pehle hi saaf kar diya tha — Rahane wapas aayenge.
Isse pehle bhi kuch batsman triple century ke baad agla Test nahi khele the. Len Hutton. Inzamam-ul-Haq.
Lekin wo chotil the.
Karun bilkul fit the. Aur phir bhi bahar.
Lekin ek baat saaf samajh lijiye — Rahane ne kuch galat nahi kiya.
Wo senior the. Saalon se team mein the. Team ne anubhav chuna. Bas.
Aur ye sirf main nahi keh raha. Karun ke apne pita ne kaha tha — "Rahane ko chunna sahi tha."
Aur Karun ne khud kaha — "Ek match, kisi aur khiladi ki do saal ki mehnat par bhaari nahi pad sakta."
Koi saazish nahi. Koi khalnayak nahi.
Lekin asli baat ye thi —
303 banane wale aadmi ko bithaane ke liye, kisi ko unse bura hona hi nahi pada.
Bas kisi ke paas wo cheez honi chahiye thi, jo Karun ke paas nahi thi — samay.
